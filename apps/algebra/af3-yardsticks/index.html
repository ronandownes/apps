Two yardsticks — sum and product plus one

Keep the first and last coefficients the same. Rearrange the four coefficients and compare the middle.

Choose positive integers
Sum case
(7x + 3)(x + 1)
7x² + 10x + 3
7 + 3 = 10
Product plus one
(7x + 1)(x + 3)
7x² + 22x + 3
7 × 3 + 1 = 22
Difference between the middle coefficients
(7 − 1)(3 − 1) = 12

The product-plus-one middle is larger by 12.

Product plus one is the maximum for fixed positive integer exterior coefficients. The sum is the smaller of these two arrangements, not necessarily the global minimum.
